Kairos iOS · Last updated May 22, 2026

About Kairos

Kairos is an iOS app that simulates a council of historical advisors to help users think through personal dilemmas. All AI reasoning happens on-device using Apple's Foundation Models framework. Your dilemmas, conversations, and history never leave your device.

What Kairos collects

  • Product interaction events (anonymous): which screens you open, which counselors you select, when you complete a debate, when you start or finish a subscription. Sent to Meta (Facebook) for analytics and ad attribution.
  • Purchase history: Apple StoreKit transaction identifiers for subscription events (start trial, subscribe, renew). Used for analytics and to ensure your subscription works across devices signed into the same Apple ID.
  • IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers): only collected if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, no IDFA is collected; analytics events still flow but without device-level attribution.
  • Anonymous installation ID: a random UUID generated on first launch, stored locally, and sent with analytics events so Meta can match conversions across sessions. Not tied to your name, email, or Apple ID.

What Kairos does NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information.
  • The content of your dilemmas, debates, or conversations with counselors.
  • Your chat history (stored only locally in the app's Core Data database).
  • Your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • Any data sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, or any third-party AI provider — Kairos uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models exclusively.

Third parties that receive data

  • Meta (Facebook): receives the anonymous analytics events listed above via the Meta App Events / Conversions API endpoint (graph.facebook.com) for ad attribution and campaign optimization. Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: receives subscription transactions via StoreKit. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Anonymous analytics events are retained by Meta according to their policies. Local data on your device (dilemma history, preferences) is retained until you delete the app or clear it from within the app's settings (Settings → History → Retention).

Your rights

  • Opt out of tracking: decline the ATT prompt on first launch, or revoke it any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Kairos.
  • Delete your local data: uninstall the app, or reset from within the app (Settings → Reset onboarding).
  • Request data deletion from Meta: contact Meta directly through their privacy controls, since Kairos does not store any data server-side that we could delete on your behalf.
  • Contact us: email create@antoniomurrieta.com for any privacy question related to Kairos.

Children

Kairos is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact create@antoniomurrieta.com.

Subscription terms

Kairos offers auto-renewing subscriptions managed by Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions. Use of the app is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

Mesa — Food & Symptom Tracker iOS · Last updated November 12, 2026

About Mesa

Mesa is an iOS food-and-symptom journal for people with IBS and other gut symptoms. Everything you log — meals, meal photos, symptoms, Bristol scale entries, bowel movements, water, factors such as stress or sleep, menstrual-cycle notes, and free-text notes — is stored only on your device. The pattern engine and the doctor/dietitian report are computed on-device. Mesa has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.

Health data stays on your iPhone

  • Your health and journal content is never sent to us, to Meta, or to any other third party, and is never used for advertising or sold.
  • Apple Health (optional): if you allow it, Mesa reads your sleep data on-device to add context to your patterns. It is never transmitted anywhere. You can revoke access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Mesa.
  • Camera / photos (optional): meal photos are saved inside the app on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Reports: the PDF report is generated on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it (e.g., via AirDrop, Mail or Messages).
  • Widget: the home-screen widget reads a small summary (today's counts) from a container shared only between Mesa and its widget, on your device.
  • Reminders: notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

What Mesa collects (app activity, not health data)

  • Anonymous app events: app install/open, onboarding completed and paywall viewed — never what you log, what you ate, your symptoms, or any other content. Sent to Meta (Facebook) via the Meta SDK for analytics and ad attribution.
  • Purchase events: subscription trial/purchase events (product ID, price and currency) for analytics and ad attribution.
  • IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers): only if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, no IDFA is collected and Meta measures in aggregated, privacy-preserving mode.

Third parties

  • Meta (Facebook): receives only the app/purchase events listed above. Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: processes subscriptions via StoreKit and, if you allow it, provides on-device Health data. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Retention & deletion

Your journal stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall Mesa (uninstalling erases all of it). If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that encrypted device backup under your control. Anonymous events are retained by Meta according to its policies.

Your choices

  • Tracking: decline the ATT prompt, or change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Mesa.
  • Health, camera, notifications: all optional; manage them in iOS Settings → Mesa.
  • Questions or requests: email create@antoniomurrieta.com.

Not medical advice

Mesa shows associations, not diagnoses, and does not prescribe diets. It is not a medical device. Always consult a qualified clinician.

Children

Mesa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Subscription terms

Mesa offers an auto-renewing annual subscription with a trial, managed by Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions. Use of the app is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

Facedash iOS · Last updated November 16, 2026

About Facedash

Facedash is an endless-runner game you control with your face (head tilts and facial expressions) or, if you prefer, with classic swipes. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.

Face data (TrueDepth camera)

  • What is read: in Face mode, Facedash uses the front TrueDepth camera through Apple's ARKit face tracking to read a few numeric facial-expression values — eye blink (left and right), jaw open, eyebrow raise, smile — plus head tilt. Nothing else is read.
  • Why: only to control the game (switch lanes, jump, duck, boost, activate power-ups) and to animate the cartoon avatar that mirrors your expression. Face data is never used for identification, authentication, advertising, marketing, analytics or profiling.
  • No images: Facedash never records, stores or transmits photos, video, depth maps or a 3D model of your face. The avatar you see is a cartoon drawn from the values above, not a camera image.
  • Storage and retention: expression values are processed in real time, in memory, on your device and discarded as each frame is handled. The one exception: when a run ends, the handful of expression values from that moment are saved on your device so the cartoon "final face" can appear in your in-game gallery. Only the 24 most recent are kept; older ones are deleted automatically. These values cannot be used to identify you or to reconstruct your face.
  • Sharing: face data is never sent to us or to any third party, and never leaves your device. Facedash has no server. If you choose to share a run card, the image contains only the cartoon avatar and your score, and goes only where you send it through the iOS share sheet.
  • Deletion: uninstalling Facedash permanently erases everything it stored, including the gallery.
  • Swipe mode does not use the camera at all.

What Facedash collects

Nothing. We receive no data from Facedash. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Your scores, coins, settings, run history and gallery are stored only on your device.

Optional Apple features

  • Game Center (optional): if you are signed in to Game Center, your high score and longest run are submitted to Apple's leaderboards. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Run recording (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, iOS's ReplayKit records the game screen so you can share a run. The clip stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Retention & deletion

Everything Facedash stores stays on your device until you uninstall the app, which erases it.

Children

Facedash does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Terms

Use of the app is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). Questions: create@antoniomurrieta.com.

En español

Facedash es un juego de carreras sin fin que controlas con la cara (o deslizando el dedo). La cámara frontal TrueDepth (mediante ARKit de Apple) solo se usa para leer unos cuantos valores numéricos de expresión — parpadeo, boca, cejas, sonrisa e inclinación de la cabeza — como controles del juego y para animar el avatar de caricatura. Nunca se graban, guardan ni envían imágenes, video, mapas de profundidad ni un modelo de tu rostro, y los datos de tu rostro nunca salen de tu dispositivo ni se comparten con terceros. Stacker Rush iOS · Last updated November 16, 2026

About Stacker Rush

Stacker Rush is a one-tap arcade block-stacking game. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. This disclosure applies to version 2.1 and later, which is free to play and supported by advertising.

What stays on your device

Your scores, coins, unlocked themes, sound packs, power-ups and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • What it is: Stacker Rush shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (to continue a run or double your coins) and occasional full-screen ads between games.
  • What Google's SDK collects: an approximate location derived from your IP address, your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), how you interact with the app and with ads, and diagnostics such as crash and performance data.
  • Why: to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized.
  • Governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Campaign measurement (Meta)

  • What it is: Stacker Rush includes Meta's SDK so that, when we advertise the game on Facebook or Instagram, Meta can tell which ads led to installs. It shows nothing inside the game.
  • What is sent to Meta: that the app was installed and opened, when a run ends (with its score and game mode), in-app purchases made in the game, basic device information (model, operating system version, language, time zone), and your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking).
  • Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission: the first time you open the game, iOS asks whether Stacker Rush may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized using it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are shown a consent form before any ad is requested, with options to consent, not consent or manage your choices. You can reopen it at any time from the in-game Store > Privacy Options. If you do not consent to storing or accessing information on your device, Meta's SDK is not started at all.
  • Remove Ads: the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase stops the full-screen ads between games. Rewarded videos stay available because you choose when to watch them, so the ad SDK remains part of the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs and Remove Ads) are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we receive

We do not receive anything that identifies you. We see aggregated reports from Apple, Google and Meta, such as downloads, ad revenue and how many runs were played.

Retention & deletion

Everything Stacker Rush stores on your device is erased when you uninstall the app. Data held by Google or Meta is kept and deleted under their policies linked above; you can also reset your advertising identifier's permission in iOS Settings. For any request, write to create@antoniomurrieta.com.

Children

Stacker Rush is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Terms

Use of the app is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). Questions: create@antoniomurrieta.com.
